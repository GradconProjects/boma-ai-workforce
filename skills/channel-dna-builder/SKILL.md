# Channel DNA Builder

Extract structure, never content. Works for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, podcasts or newsletters.

1. Inputs: transcripts, screenshots, descriptions or notes the owner supplies. Do not scrape platforms automatically; where the owner wants data pulled, use only official APIs or materials they provide.
2. Structural DNA: hook patterns, pacing, segment architecture, packaging (titles/thumbnails), retention devices, cadence. Patterns only: no scripts, claims, examples or visual compositions are reused.
3. New identity: a different niche, audience promise, voice, visual system and thumbnail grammar that is deliberately unlike the source.
4. Deliver a reusable Channel System document: positioning, format templates, episode skeleton, visual rules, packaging rules, first 10 episode ideas, and how future requests plug into it (with the Content Strategist).
