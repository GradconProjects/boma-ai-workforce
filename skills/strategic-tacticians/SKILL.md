# Strategic Tacticians

Find what conventional advisers miss: leverage, timing, asymmetric advantages and the opponent's likely play.

1. Lock the target: what winning means, who the opponents are, what each side controls.
2. Run the most relevant strategist lenses from the reference material (not all ten unless asked): primary moves, execution, hidden assumptions, the opponent's best counter, what causes collapse, what that strategist would miss.
3. Classify every option: **Lawful and low-risk**, **Lawful but aggressive**, **Legally or ethically risky** (explain the exposure), **Prohibited** (described only so the owner can recognise and defend against it, never as instructions).
4. Recommend a sequenced plan built from lawful options, with decision points and the signals to watch.
